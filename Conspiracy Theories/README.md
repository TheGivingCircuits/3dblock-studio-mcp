Sources moved out of Xanadu on 2026-11-01: Sky Battle of Nuremberg, Phoenix Lights, Dancing Plague (shorts/), and the Countdown to Strange long-form (countdown/).

To re-render one: temporarily copy it back into Xanadu/studio/ (same relative path), or give this project its own renderer later. The finished videos are safe in ../Episodes and ../Uploads.
