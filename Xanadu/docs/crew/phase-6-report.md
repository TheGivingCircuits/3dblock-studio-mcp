## Why this film
It's the best-known 90s slasher and the channel hasn't done it. Its beats read in low-poly without any trademark design: a cordless phone, stovetop popcorn, a boy tied on a patio, a garage door, a movie fan's rules, two killers, a falling TV. The killer is our own figure: a black hooded robe and a bone-white mask with two eye slits and no mouth.
