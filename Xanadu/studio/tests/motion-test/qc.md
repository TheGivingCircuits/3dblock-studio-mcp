# QC: Motion Test - Take 01

Verdict: FAIL

Method: contact sheets of the whole take at 2 fps (7 sheets of 9 s each) plus full-frame stills at 1.5, 4.5, 7.5, 10.5, 43.5 and 57 s. I did not step every boundary at 4-6 fps, so blend notes are from the 2 fps sheets only and a one-frame pop could be missed. I did not run the engine clipping check.

## Overall

For most of the take the character is either lying or pitched forward, small, in the lower part of the frame, or partly off-frame. The whole take is a heavy orange-brown with low contrast. The uniform reads grey, not blue. Cap, moustache and face are not readable until the last idle (about 52-60 s). The standing pose the owner called "weird" is not fixed in this take: idle, talk, wave, point, phone call, look around and swing all show the torso tipped forward past 45 degrees. The only upright, readable standing pose is the final "back to idle".

## Blockers

1. Standing pose (idle, talk, wave, point, phone call, swing) is pitched far forward. The final idle shows a correct upright pose, so the pose is possible but is not used for the first 57 s. This is the exact fix the take was meant to prove.
2. Framing. Lean is fully hidden by the pump. Walk, run, sneak, sit, drive, climb and grab show the character off-frame, tiny, or hidden. The camera does not follow him.
3. Props that are not reached. Wheel (no hands), ladder (no contact), cup (not picked up) and chair seat (not confirmed).
4. Feet and ground in the idle family. Boots are visibly separated from the legs, and the shadow is offset, so he looks to float. Crawl looks sunk.
5. Lighting and colour. The whole frame is a low-contrast orange-brown, the uniform reads grey, and the face and cap are unreadable.

## Not judged

- Smoothness of every blend. Visible at 2 fps: lean to talk, fall to ragdoll and ragdoll to idle all cut between very different poses in under 0.5 s, and the first frame of fall is a large jump from crawl. Confirm at full frame rate.
- Hair, cap and moustache movement with the head, since they are not readable in most frames.
