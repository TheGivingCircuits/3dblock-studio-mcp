## The hidden clue
A little twig stick man hanging off a porch in the town, at about 0:10 ("everyone tells them the same thing").

## The clue ladder
1. Three film students, a documentary about a local legend, locals warning them off.
2. The lost map and the creek they keep coming back to.
3. Something circling the tent at night; stick figures in the trees and piles of stones at dawn.
4. One of them vanishes; the bundle of twigs wrapped in his shirt.
5. The giveaway last: the house in the woods with handprints on the walls, and him standing in the corner of the basement.
