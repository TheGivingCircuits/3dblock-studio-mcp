**Fact-checked against the film** (film wiki, 2026-10-23)
- **The trip:** a brother and sister driving home from college for spring break through the Florida countryside in a 1960 Chevy, playing a game of guessing what vanity plates mean.
- **The truck:** a rusty old delivery truck (a 1941 Chevy cab-over) tailgates them, then passes. The **brother** reads the plate, BEATNGU, and guesses "beating you". It means "be eating you".
- **The church:** the truck is parked by an abandoned church, where a hulking man in a long coat slides bodies wrapped in old sheets down a big pipe. The brother insists on going back, looks into the pipe while his sister holds his legs, and slides down.
- **The House of Pain:** a cavern beneath the church with hundreds of preserved bodies sewn into the walls.
- **The warning:** at a diner the payphone rings. A psychic warns the brother, plays the old song down the line, and tells them to run when they hear it.
- **The scarecrow:** at an old woman's farm the creature hides as a scarecrow in the yard, then reveals its wings.
- **The rules:** it rises every 23rd spring, for 23 days, to feed, and picks its victims by the smell of their fear.
- **The ending:** at the police station it sniffs them both, tosses the sister aside, chooses the brother and flies off with him through the window.
